# ADR 0001: Explicit chunks and separate visual/collision artifacts

Status: accepted, 2026-09-14.

The world must remain stable under camera changes and revisits. Video synthesis alone cannot satisfy this. Store stable chunks and separate Gaussian PLY visuals from GLB solid proxies. Start with deterministic authored fixtures rendered by PlayCanvas; generation adapters later supply the same artifact boundary. Portal doors control streaming seams. The browser never reaches the worker directly. SQLite and content-addressed local artifacts are owned by the Mac orchestrator. This gives up arbitrary seamless geometry in favor of a measurable walkable prototype and keeps CUDA/model internals off the product boundary.
