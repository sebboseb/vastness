# Generated passage experiment

Question: can generated geometry provide the actual supported floor and free volume traversed by a player? Existing intention pipeline, M0 and M1 remain intact. No new intention UX or gameplay. Prototype runtime stays on codex/generated-passage-prototype; integration receives evidence and documentation only.

Use the current committed SDXL/TRELLIS pipeline first, with a small declared batch of scene-oriented requests (archway, tunnel, chamber/courtyard) and no candidate-specific collider edits. Persist original intent and compiled semantic constraints through the existing intention service. Keep GPU jobs serial and retain every failed candidate. Assess source image, raw GLB, simplified collision and browser appearance separately. If three or more varied candidates consistently lack usable generated support/free space, stop prompt fishing and benchmark WorldGrow and HunyuanWorld from official sources against the same navigation requirement and actual RTX 3090 capacity. Do not download new large models until sizes and E-backed storage budget are established.

A passing route must enter the generated mesh footprint and traverse a meaningful opening/interior, remain supported by generated surface triangles (not an authored floor), have radius 0.3 m body clearance and 1.8 m headroom, bounded slope/step height, and a reversible continuous route of at least 3 m through generated space. Test against original surface geometry as well as derived collision; do not clear occupancy, delete faces or hand-author floor patches to make a route pass. Rigid orientation and declared uniform scale are permitted, applied identically to visual and collision artifacts and recorded. The approach/threshold may be authored up to the seam, but no invisible destination support floor is allowed. Failure keeps that candidate explicitly failed.

Geometry assessment publishes an auditable route, foot support samples/triangle identities, clearance checks, dimensions and reasoned rejection metrics. Movement uses the same generated support/clearance model as validation and includes meaningful gap, wall, low ceiling, step and return tests. A disposable browser view loads hash-verified accepted artifacts, crosses from the scaffold onto generated support, moves through it and returns; restart preserves identities, route and visits. No teleportation or prerecorded camera animation substitutes for actual collision-constrained movement.

Measured opening evidence requires at least 1 m contiguous enclosure along the accepted route: either roof coverage across all nine footprint probes at 1.8–6 m above the feet, or opposing torso-height walls. A small floating patch or distant roof must fail. Exterior scaffold support is bounded to the outside of the measured seam and carries negative support IDs; all accepted interior route samples must use only original nonnegative triangle IDs. Collision is a spatial index over every original triangle, avoiding the prior conservative voxel shell's false closures without carving candidate-specific openings.

Parallel art experiment: transfer coarse color/material cues from generated Gaussian DC colors to abstract mesh, with a small stable palette and no geometry/collision changes. Compare uniform solid, coarse generated color and original splat at matched poses on the existing mushroom/crystal artifacts and any accepted passage. Do not claim recovered material physics/emission from color alone. New helpers only; existing experiment rendering remains unchanged.
